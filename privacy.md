
# Privacy Policy for Repit

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Repit – Workout Tracker respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

Repit does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer.

Workout data entered into the app is stored locally on your device and remains under your control.

## Third-Party Services

Repit does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

Purchases made through the App Store are processed by Apple. Repit does not receive or store your payment information.

## Data Export and Import

Repit may allow you to export and import your workout data. Exported files are created at your request and remain under your control.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Repit's functionality changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
